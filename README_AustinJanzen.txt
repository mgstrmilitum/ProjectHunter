This was a group midterm project which I worked on with a team of 4 other teammates. We had worked on a smaller project the previous month and decided to start fresh for our midterm on a new design, which is a sort of FPS Van Helsing-core thing. This was our first major collaborative project at Full Sail and while we had some stressful moments, I am pretty happy with where it ended up overall. I started collaborating with one other student on the player movement system, and once we finished that, I worked on the first castle level (we ended up with 5 levels all together) as far as design, placement of enemies, and so on. I also aided our AI guy who was having trouble getting the animation system to work with the enemy attacks, so I took over a substantial amount of the enemy animation system. I also did some substantial work on the final boss arena level, as well as some tuning on the vertical climber level, and did the functionality and placement of our 'hidden star' garlic pickups.

All-in-all, this is the project I am most proud of that is 100% finished at the time of my portfolio submission (still working on my final project). Not only did we get a full game experience with a lot of interesting features, we all really grew as developers and teammates throughout the process. Shoutout to my guys Sam, Luke, Jason, and Light!
